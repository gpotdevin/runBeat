RunBeat is a BPM-based audio player made for runners. It synchronizes your music with your running cadence by adjusting the tempo of your songs without changing the pitch, so every step lands on the beat.

KEY FEATURES:

• TEMPO MATCHING
Speed up or slow down tracks without pitch distortion, using the SoundTouch time-stretching engine. Match playback to your cadence automatically.

• CADENCE TARGETS
Set your target step frequency (default 172 BPM, range 160-195 BPM). RunBeat continuously adjusts playback speed to keep your rhythm while you run. Choose how the beat divides — Binary (1x/2x) or Ternary (1.5x/3x) — so every step lands in time with the music.

• BPM DETECTION
Automatic tempo detection powered by aubio analyzes your library, tap along manually with the tap-to-beat tool, or import pre-analyzed BPM values from a CSV file (Settings → Advanced).

• PLAYBACK
Full library browsing with playlists and BPM metadata. Playback continues in the background with a media notification and Bluetooth controls, so you can run hands-free.

• OFFLINE-FIRST
Your music, BPM data, and playlists live in a local Room database. No account, no tracking, no data collection.

RunBeat is open source (GPLv3). Source: https://github.com/gpotdevin/runBeat
