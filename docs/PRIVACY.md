# RunBeat Privacy Note

**Last updated: 2026-10-17**

RunBeat is an offline-first, BPM-based audio player for runners. This note explains what data the app handles and which permissions it uses.

## Data collection

**RunBeat does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**

- No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs.
- The app does not even declare the `INTERNET` permission: no network requests are made on your behalf, and the app works fully offline.
- Your music library, BPM values, playlists, and settings are stored **only on your device**, in a local Room database.
- Nothing leaves your device. There is no server-side component operated by the developer.

## Permissions

The table below matches the permissions actually declared in [`app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`](../app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml).

| Permission | Why RunBeat requests it |
|---|---|
| `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` (Android 13+) | Read your audio files so they can be listed, played, and analyzed. |
| `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 12 and below, `maxSdkVersion=32`) | Legacy equivalent of `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` for older Android versions. |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 10 and below, `maxSdkVersion=29`) | Legacy permission for writing BPM values back into audio file metadata (ID3 `TBPM` frame) on very old Android versions. On Android 11 and above, metadata writes use per-file MediaStore write requests instead. The app never modifies files you did not select. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` / `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK` | Keeps music playing in the background while you run, when the screen is off or another app is in front. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (Android 13+) | Shows the media playback notification with playback controls. |

`RECORD_AUDIO`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` and `MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS` are **not** declared by RunBeat. In particular, the app never uses the microphone; BPM detection analyzes the audio files themselves.

Two additional permissions appear in the merged manifest at build time, without being declared by the app:

- `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — added by the Media3 library, which inspects connectivity as part of its playback logic. RunBeat itself makes no network requests.
- `DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION` — a marker permission generated by the Android Gradle Plugin for dynamically registered receivers. It grants no capabilities and involves no data access.

No permission is used to collect or send data off-device. File-access permissions are requested at runtime only when the relevant feature is used, and can be denied without breaking core playback.

## Children's privacy

RunBeat is a general-purpose music tool and is not directed at children. No data is collected from anyone, regardless of age.

## Changes to this note

Material changes to data handling or permissions will be reflected in this document. Since no data is collected, the practical impact of any change is limited to how on-device permissions are used.
